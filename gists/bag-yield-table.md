# Concrete bag yield table

Reference data published by **buildcalcs.io** (https://buildcalcs.io). All yields assume the recommended water content; check the bag you actually bought before ordering.

| Bag size | Yield per bag | Bags per cubic yard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 40 lb | 0.30 cu ft | 90 |
| 50 lb | 0.375 cu ft | 72 |
| 60 lb | 0.45 cu ft | 60 |
| 80 lb | 0.60 cu ft | 45 |
| 90 lb | 0.675 cu ft | 40 |

UK/Australia dry mix: 25 kg = 0.0117 m³ (about 85 bags per m³); 20 kg = 0.00936 m³ (about 107 bags per m³).

Fast-Setting concrete: 50 lb ≈ 0.375 cu ft, 80 lb ≈ 0.60 cu ft. Sets in 20–40 minutes — not a slab mix.

A 10 × 10 ft slab at 4 in thick is 33.33 cu ft = 1.23 yd³ = 56 bags of 80 lb before waste; add 5% and buy 59.

Sources: Quikrete Concrete Mix product page; Quikrete Fast-Setting data sheet 1004-50; NIST SP 811. Checked 2026-11-22.
