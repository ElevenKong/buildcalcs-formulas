# Cubic yard chart for common slabs

Worked volumes from **buildcalcs.io** (https://buildcalcs.io). Thickness is converted from inches to feet before multiplying; volume in cubic feet is divided by 27 for cubic yards.

| Slab | 4 in | 5 in | 6 in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 × 10 ft | 1.23 yd³ | 1.54 yd³ | 1.85 yd³ |
| 12 × 12 ft | 1.78 yd³ | 2.22 yd³ | 2.67 yd³ |
| 20 × 20 ft | 4.94 yd³ | 6.17 yd³ | 7.41 yd³ |

At 4 in thick one cubic yard covers 81 sq ft; at 3 in it covers 108 sq ft; at 6 in it covers 54 sq ft. Coverage = 27 ÷ thickness in feet.

A 10 × 10 ft slab at 4 in is 33.33 cu ft ÷ 27 = 1.23 yd³, or 56 bags of 80 lb mix. These are net volumes — add 5–10% before ordering.

Source for the conversion: NIST SP 811 (https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811). Checked 2026-11-22.
